When Stars Fade

When Stars Fade

Is a modern progressive metal band from Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, formed in 2024 by experienced musicians with over two decades of musical background.

Blending intricate progressive arrangements, powerful guitar-driven soundscapes, and atmospheric elements, the band creates a distinctive sonic identity that balances technical complexity with emotional depth and melodic intensity.

Drawing on years of musical experience, When Stars Fade combines modern metal aesthetics with a dynamic and expressive approach to songwriting. Their music explores contrasts between heaviness and atmosphere, aggression and melody, precision and raw emotion.

Although formed in 2024, the band brings together musicians whose individual journeys span more than 20 years, resulting in a mature musical vision and a strong foundation for further creative exploration.

With a commitment to evolving their sound and delivering an intense, immersive live experience, When Stars Fade aims to establish its presence on the international modern metal scene.
